Product Details

Cappuccino is a popular Italian coffee beverage made with a shot of espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of milk foam on top. It has a balanced taste—strong from the espresso but smooth and creamy due to the milk. The drink is usually served in a small cup, around 150–180 ml, and often topped with a sprinkle of cocoa or cinnamon.

  • Made with 1/3 espresso, 1/3 steamed milk, and 1/3 milk foam
  • Known for its rich, bold flavor and velvety texture
  • Typically enjoyed in the morning or as a midday pick-me-up
  • Often paired with croissants or light pastries
  • Served hot, but iced versions are also popular
  • Named after the Capuchin monks due to the similar color of their robes

This coffee is ideal for those who like strong coffee but enjoy a creamy finish.
